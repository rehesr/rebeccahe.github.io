About Me

2024-05-02

My name is Shunran (Rebecca) He, and I am currently a Master of Financial Engineering student at Cornell University’s School of Operations Research and Information Engineering. I obtained my undergraduate degree from Peking University’s School of Economics, majoring in finance.

Professional Interests

I am interested in pursuing a career in quantitative finance, leveraging my strong foundation in advanced coursework and hands-on internship experience. My academic training not only includes mathematical and programming courses such as Stochastic Analysis, Monte Carlo Simulation, Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence, and Data Structure and Algorithm, but also financial application courses such as Fixed Income Securities, Financial Derivatives, Financial Modeling and Quantitative Methods, Financial Risk Management. During internships at Shengshi Investment, Luxtech Capital, and Commando Capital, I developed high-frequency trading signals, optimized multi-factor portfolios using ML techniques, and designed strategies yielding annual excess returns of up to 17%. These experiences have equipped me with the technical skills (Python, C++, MATLAB) and quantitative intuition to thrive in roles like quant research or algorithmic trading.

Personal Interests

I would like to consider myself as a cinephile, but in reality, I have very limited knowledge of movies. My favorite television series is Succession, because I relate to the feeling of desperation and disappointment. I play both the piano and the guitar, however only at an elementary level.
